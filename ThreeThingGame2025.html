The Catcher Of The Snakes

The Catchers of the Snakes: An Edmund Fitzgerald can be found here on itch.io

What is this game?

This is the very first game jam I have entered and I have entered with two of my IRL friends Mak and Ben. Said gamejam is called Three Thing Game 2025 where you have three random words and you have to build a game around it. Our three words are Snakes, Sending, Oceanliners.

What is going on?

The Catchers of the snakes is all about catching dangerous snakes that have escaped through an ocean liner and grabbing upgrades along the way.

How was it made?

The gameplay is rather simple and the game was all made in unity. I mainly worked on the menus and UI of the game while my friends worked on the rest of the game and gameplay. I didn't expect for us to win this especially for my first game jam entry but this brought me enough motivation to try and join other gamejams and make more games to improve my programming and other skills.

In any case I hope you like the game that we made and any other games I will make in the future!
