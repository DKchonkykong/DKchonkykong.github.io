Rare Oddities

This page is about an oddity I own and the story behind it all.

So, a few months after 2025, while I was at a boring lecture on the powerpoint I noticed a strange ultrawide monitor that I have never saw before. Jokingly I said that this was my gamer set up from the 2000s but then at the back of my mind I was thinking. What's the source of this image? Who made this image? What year was it made? Well, Curiosity got the better of me so I ended up trying to find more information on this strange and mysterious monitor that looked to be from the late 90s early 2000s. So me and my friend Matt tried to find articles, reverse image searching, anything about this odd monitor and we couldn't find much other than being a triple monitor set up inside one monitor and that there wasn't that much publication over it. We knew it costed a lot of money at the time ($20,000) or about $35k in todays money, we know it was used for buisness applications and we got a lead on the name of the monitor which is the Panoram PV290 DSK. Although, we did notice an ebay listing for a magazine that included the monitor in a magazine. As you can see it's from a german sounding publication of Hammacher Schlemacher and it mentions winter 2001 catalogue. So we know know that the latest it could be is from 2001. But that seller is from the US there was only one copy available so who would be stupid enough to buy it.

Well desprate for any resolution and answers I ended up (not going to japan) buying the magazine.. It costed me quite a bit due to delivery fees but I figured it might be the only way to figure out what this thing is (that and I might've watched too much nick robinson tbh). The magazine came all the way from Ohio too which is crazy and it seems to be in pretty good condition. But the main reason why I bought it was that there was no real achives of it not on archive.org either so I want to try to scan all of the pages I got and archive it on my own. Originally I wanted to make this into a youtube video but due to being busy with school and personal life I just didn't have the time. Hopefully you're into odd tech as I am and understand a bit more about these wierd tech oddities.
